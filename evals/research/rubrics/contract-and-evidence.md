# Research contract and evidence grader

Pass only when the raw transcript and retained artifact show that one exact discovery artifact was validated and emitted in full by the deterministic preflight before target inspection, no target inspection preceded the approved scope-tracer Run, every approved question is covered exactly once, current-code claims use valid clickable file-line evidence with repository-relative labels, Git is treated only as precedent, external web research is recorded as deferred, and only one research artifact path exists. In the revision case, require independent observations proving that same path passed inspection before and after Revise with two different hashes; Accept must freeze the second hash without creating another artifact or workflow sidecar. Require at most four direct children total: one tracer and no more than three fresh analysis children. Fail on recommendations, invented citations, hidden fan-out, child reuse, duplicate research artifacts, sidecars, product-source changes, or successor-stage creation. Return a binary pass or fail with concise evidence.
